ABOUT THE STUDY

Geometry remembers.

Drawn from the supplied Uzbek ornament, each ceramic square keeps its own place in the pattern. A traveling wave loosens the geometry into curved streams before every piece finds its way home.

Move over the mosaic to disturb its surface. Choose a state below — assembled, unraveling, in flow or returning — or click the mosaic to step to the next one. Keys 1–4 select a state; ← → switch ornaments.

Reduced motion follows your system preference.
